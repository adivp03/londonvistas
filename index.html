How the fit score works. Fit = (Interest × 40%) + (Acclaim × 60%). Interest rewards overlap with history, politics, science, art, design and music (with your specific taste in Western classical, Indian classical, neo-classical, ambient, lo-fi and world music), and gives a lift to anything cross-cultural or non-Western. Acclaim starts from a standard host-prestige stand-in of 50 for anything not yet reviewed, and adds 10 for a genuinely good external review or canonical/masterwork status — mixed reviews stay at 50. Not-yet-reviewed events keep an informational flag (⚠) but are never scored down for it, and the strongest of them are gathered under strong fit, not yet reviewed at the foot of the page. Badges (Cross-cultural, Free, Closing soon, premieres, sold-out) are information only and never change the ranking.

Coverage notes for this week. Several autumn and winter programmes were still unpublished when this was gathered and are under-represented here: the Science Museum has no Lates on sale for the window; the Natural History Museum's autumn Lates date could not be confirmed; Conway Hall's full autumn programme, Intelligence Squared and How To Academy returned no confirmed in-window events; and the Royal Society's and universities' October–December lists were only partially visible. LSE Public Events had confirmed autumn talks, but they were policy and international-affairs titles that fell outside the science remit. The BBC Proms are absent by calendar rather than oversight — the 2026 season closed on 12 September, before this window opens. Whole-venue gaps this week: Somerset House (the 1-54 African Art Fair could not be pinned to firm dates and was left out rather than guessed), the Donmar Warehouse (its December premiere's title was unconfirmed), Genesis Cinema and the Curzon repertory strand (no dated in-window listing), and Café OTO and the London Review Bookshop are represented by a single confirmed event each. On sourcing: direct page fetches to nearly every venue domain were blocked by this environment's network policy, so this week's research rests on live search results quoting venue pages and press rather than the pages themselves. Where a price or a specific event page could not be confirmed it is shown as "Ticketed — see listing" or links to a search rather than being invented.
