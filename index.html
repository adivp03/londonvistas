How the star rating works. Every event is rated out of 5 stars from five signals, computed uniformly: raw = (2 × Venue) + Creator + Reviewed + Theme + Upcoming (max 6), then stars = round(raw / 6 × 5 × 2) / 2. Venue (×2) scores 1 if the venue is a named institution on our checklist. Creator scores 1 if a field-leading artist, director, performer, composer, author or academic is centrally involved. Reviewed scores 1 for a rave / five-star reception, 0.5 for a solid four-star notice, and 0 when mixed, below four stars or not yet reviewed (for talks, the speaker's authority stands in). Theme scores 1 — capped, no matter how many it hits — for touching history, politics, science, art, design or a liked music genre (Western classical, Indian classical, neo-classical, ambient, lo-fi, world, indie-rock, singer-songwriter). Upcoming scores 1 only on concrete evidence the creator is a genuine breakout. Cross-cultural is an informational badge only and earns no points. Not-yet-reviewed events keep an informational flag (⚠) but are never scored down for it, and the strongest of them gather under Fresh & emerging. Info badges (On now, Closing soon, Free, Cross-cultural, premieres) never change the rating; the rating only ranks — nothing is filtered out.
