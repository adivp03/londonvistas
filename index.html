How the fit score works. Fit = (Interest × 40%) + (Acclaim × 60%). Interest rewards overlap with history, politics, science, art, design and music (with your specific taste in Western classical, Indian classical, neo-classical, ambient, lo-fi and world music), and gives a lift to anything cross-cultural or non-Western. Acclaim starts from a standard host-prestige stand-in of 50 for anything not yet reviewed, and adds 10 for a genuinely good external review or canonical/masterwork status — mixed reviews stay at 50. Not-yet-reviewed events keep an informational flag (⚠) but are never scored down for it, and the strongest of them are gathered under strong fit, not yet reviewed at the foot of the page. Badges (Cross-cultural, Free, Closing soon, premieres, sold-out) are information only and never change the ranking.

Coverage notes for this week. Several autumn and winter programmes were still unpublished when this was gathered, and are not represented here: the Royal Society's public-events titles for October and November; Imperial College's Lates series, confirmed only as "returning in autumn 2026"; the Science Museum's Lates, with none currently on sale for the October–December window; the Natural History Museum's Lates dates; Conway Hall's full autumn Ethical Matters programme; and a talks strand at the Barbican for the post-September window. The BBC Proms are absent by calendar rather than oversight — the 2026 season closed on 12 September, before this window opens. Coverage of commercial galleries is deliberately thin, and Genesis, Ciné Lumière, the Curzon repertory strand and the London Korean Film Festival could not be confirmed with dated listings and were left out rather than guessed. Two further Wigmore Hall and ENO concerts with confirmed dates were dropped because no reliable event page could be established for them. On sourcing: direct page fetches to nearly every venue domain were blocked by this environment's network policy, so this week's research rests on live search results quoting venue pages and press releases rather than on the pages themselves. Where a price could not be confirmed it is shown as "Ticketed — see listing" or "price TBC" rather than invented, and a handful of events link to a search rather than a deep link because no event page could be resolved. Please treat times and prices as provisional and check the listing.
