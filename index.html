How the rating works. Each event scores out of 5 stars from five signals: an institutional venue (🏛, counts double), a notable creator (👤), strong reviews (✅ — four stars = half, a rave = full), theme fit (🎯 — history, politics, science, art, design, or music in a genre you like; hitting any one scores once), and an up-and-coming creator with real buzz (🌱). Badges (On now, Closing soon, Free, Cross-cultural) are information only and never change the rating.
